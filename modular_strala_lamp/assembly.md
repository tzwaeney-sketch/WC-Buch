# Assembly Guide

A 20-step sequence to build a complete lamp from printed parts.

## Before you start

- Confirm `passage_test_ring(60)` passes your real plug.
- Confirm the chosen bayonet tolerance pair twists and locks cleanly.
- Have the STRÅLA cord set, a 200 g steel ballast ring, 4 rubber feet, and
  M3 flat-head screws ready.

## Steps

1. Lay out base, modules, top cap, shade, and the STRÅLA cord set.
2. Drop the steel ballast ring into the base weight chamber (annular pocket).
3. Seat the `lamp_base_weight_insert` over the ballast if not using loose steel.
4. Route the STRÅLA cable up through the base 60 mm bore from the bottom.
5. Lay the cable into the rear cable channel and out the side exit hole.
6. Press the `cable_exit_grommet` into the side exit hole around the cable.
7. Attach the 4 rubber feet into the foot pockets on the underside.
8. Fasten the `lamp_base_bottom_plate` with 3 × M3 flat-head screws.
9. Because every bore is >= 60 mm, the plug passes through with no rewiring.
10. Thread the plug/cable up through the first decorative module bore.
11. Seat the module female bayonet onto the base male bayonet.
12. Twist 25° until it locks (you feel the lugs reach the groove end).
13. Repeat steps 10–12 for each subsequent module, threading cable as you go.
14. Keep total body height in the 570–630 mm target window.
15. Thread the cable through the `mod_top_cap` bore last.
16. Lock the top cap onto the uppermost module (female bayonet only — terminal).
17. Fit the STRÅLA socket into the top cap pocket; secure with the split clamp.
18. Tighten the split clamp halves with 2 × M3 screws per joint.
19. Snap the chosen shade's mounting ring onto the top-cap/STRÅLA interface.
20. Plug in, confirm the bulb seats, and run the stability check (see
    `stability_report.md`).

## Disassembly

Reverse the twist on each bayonet (25° back) and lift. The cable never needs
to be cut — pull the plug back down through the bores.
